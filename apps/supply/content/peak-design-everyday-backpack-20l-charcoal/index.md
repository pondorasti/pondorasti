Was fun for about a year, and then got bored of it.

It just feels way too bulky for everyday use, and don’t like how it doesn’t stand on it’s own. Tends to always fall forward due to it’s rounded shape.

The internal origami layout is perfect for bringing along my Kinesis Advantage360 Bluetooth and Apple Magic Trackpad 2.

Not a camera person, yet I still find it a great backpack for travel. Bonus points for the carry-on strap.
