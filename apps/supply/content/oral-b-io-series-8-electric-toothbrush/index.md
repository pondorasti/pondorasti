For some reason, when I received my first software engineering paycheck, I thought it would be a good idea to get the most expensive toothbrush money can buy.

Never been more wrong in my life.

DO NOT get the fancy ass toothbrush with a screen, and 8 speed shifter. Not worth the money, and have never used once any of the extra features it provides.

Other than that, electric toothbrushes are great. All it needs is an on/off button.

Credit where credit is due, the green/red pressure LED is pretty cool.
